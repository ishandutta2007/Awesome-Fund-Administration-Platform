## 🌐 SaaS / Hosted Platforms Market Overview

> 📊 **Estimated Market Size & Industry Concentration**:
> The global Fund Administration Software market is estimated at **~$3.8 Billion to $4.2 Billion (2026)**, expanding at a CAGR of ~8.5%. The market is **moderately fragmented**, featuring giant enterprise financial institution consolidators (SS&C, FIS, BlackRock eFront) alongside fast-growing modern SaaS challengers (Juniper Square, Carta, Allvue). While top enterprise players hold dominant market share among tier-1 mega-funds, specialized mid-market and venture-focused platforms create space for high-growth SaaS alternatives.

# Awesome-Fund-Administration-Platform

## Top Fund Administration Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Private Fund Accounting, Investor Reporting, NAV, Capital Calls, Distributions & LP Portals*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Fund Administration**. These systems support private equity, venture capital, real estate, and alternative fund accounting, investor statements, capital activity, waterfall calculations, and LP reporting.



**Examples** include Juniper Square, eFront, Allvue, Investran (FIS), FundCount, SS&C Geneva, Altvia, Dynamo Software, Paxus, Carta Fund Administration, Alter Domus, SEI Archway, and Yardi Investment Manager (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full institutional fund administration is almost entirely commercial. Practical open options are limited to experimental portfolio/fund-ops platforms and data-backbone projects. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the large commercial gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Juniper Square](https://www.junipersquare.com/)**  

  Software-enabled fund administration platform for private funds—investor portal, accounting, capital activity, and LP services popular with emerging and mid-market managers.



- **[eFront (BlackRock)](https://www.efront.com/)**  

  Enterprise alternative investment platform covering fund accounting, portfolio monitoring, and investor reporting for PE, real estate, and private markets.



- **[Allvue Systems](https://www.allvue.com/)**  

  Private capital suite for fund accounting, portfolio monitoring, investor portals, and end-to-end fund lifecycle management.



- **[FIS Investran / Private Capital Suite](https://www.fisglobal.com/)**  

  Established fund accounting and administration platform used by PE firms and administrators for partnership accounting, allocations, and investor reporting.



- **[FundCount](https://www.fundcount.com/)**  

  Fund accounting and portfolio management software with strong reporting, NAV, capital statements, and investor portal capabilities for PE and alternatives.



- **[SS&C Geneva](https://www.ssctech.com/)**  

  Multi-asset fund accounting platform used for complex portfolios, daily NAV, and institutional fund administration.



- **[Altvia](https://www.altvia.com/)**  

  Private equity CRM and fund operations platform focused on LP relationship management, fundraising, and portfolio support.



- **[Dynamo Software](https://www.dynamosoftware.com/)**  

  Alternative investment management platform for PE, real estate, and private markets covering CRM, portfolio, and investor workflows.



- **[Carta Fund Administration](https://carta.com/)**  

  Fund administration and equity management services/platform aimed at venture and private funds, with investor reporting and cap-table adjacency.



- **[Alter Domus / SEI Archway / Yardi Investment Manager and other administrators](https://www.example.com/)**  

  Leading fund administration service providers and platforms used by institutional managers for full-service admin, accounting, and reporting.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Prototyping portfolio monitoring and basic books with experimental open platforms before engaging a commercial admin.

- Using open data-backbone approaches (e.g., AMOS-style) to own warehouse data while still relying on commercial admin for NAV and LP statements.

- Extending open ERP accounting for very early-stage or single-vehicle funds.

- Accepting that audit-ready multi-fund accounting, complex waterfalls, regulatory reporting, and institutional LP portals still require commercial platforms or full-service administrators (Juniper Square, Allvue, Investran, eFront, SS&C, FundCount, Carta, Alter Domus, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership and transparency rather than replacing licensed fund admin.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Track deals and portfolio in open or commercial CRM → maintain books in open ERP or commercial fund accounting → generate statements via commercial admin or custom reports → deliver to LPs via portal. Suitable only for limited-scope or experimental use. Most institutional and emerging managers that raise external capital use commercial fund administration software or outsourced administrators for credibility and control.
